Privacy Policy

Stack to Orbit · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Stack to Orbit ("the game") is a one-tap stacking game published by boidbit. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not send any data from your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in your device's local storage: your levels and stars, high scores, unlocked skins, statistics, and your settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion, height meter). This data never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Device features

  • Sound: played on your device for game effects.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback, and can be turned off in Settings.

The game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or social SDKs, and does not share data with third parties. If you download it from an app store, that store (for example Google Play) handles the download and has its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
